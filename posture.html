Posture

Why this surface exists.

Foundation is the public posture layer for body and place under One Mission — a garden stance, not a product funnel.

Adam garden

The work begins with a simple claim: life is meant to proliferate under wise limits. Stewardship of body and dwelling is part of that claim — ordinary, durable, non-theatrical.

We write for people who already know that sleep, light, movement, and place matter, and who refuse both medical cosplay and empty optimization culture.

HOG as intellectual discipline only

Where HOG physics appears in the wider One Mission network, it is an intellectual discipline — a way of thinking carefully about systems, judgment, and coherence.

It is not a proof of any diet, training plan, or supplement. Foundation will never use physics language to smuggle medical authority. That boundary is permanent; see Refused.
