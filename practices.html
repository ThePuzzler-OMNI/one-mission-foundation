Practices

Orientation, not prescription.

Five lenses for daily life. Nothing here diagnoses, treats, or sells a cure. If a sentence feels like medical advice, defer to clinicians and to Refused.

01 Flip times

Protect the transitions: wake, first light, last light, sleep. The quality of those edges often does more than heroic midday effort. Prefer consistent clocks over perfect ones.

02 Movement

Move as a creature with a body — load, walk, breathe, recover. Prefer practices you can keep for decades over extremes that require an audience.

03 Recovery

Sleep is the primary recovery instrument. Quiet rooms, honest dark, and fewer midnight inputs beat elaborate recovery products that cannot replace rest.

04 Substances

Caffeine, alcohol, and other inputs have real costs and real uses. Count them as design choices in a day, not as identity. We do not sell stacks or cures.

05 Nutrition

Eat in a way that sustains work, family, and long attention. We will not baptize any single diet with physics language or medical theater. Personal medical questions belong with qualified care.
